What we do

Our work spans four pillars of community wellness, each designed to meet people where they are.

Mental Health Awareness & Psychological Support

Angaza Wellness Foundation promotes mental well-being through counselling, psychosocial support, mental health awareness and safe spaces. We reach individuals, families, students, couples, organisations and communities, while supporting youth, survivors of gender-based violence, inmates and former inmates through programmes that promote healing, resilience, life skills and healthier communities.

Community Empowerment

Angaza empowers individuals and communities by connecting mental wellness with practical opportunities for growth. Through EWIP, we provide skills, mentorship, career guidance, education and meaningful connections that help people build confidence, livelihoods, purpose and pathways toward a more stable and fulfilling future.

Youth Mentorship & Development

Angaza empowers young people through mentorship, workshops and Teens Camps that support self-awareness, mental wellness, relationships, career development, leadership and personal growth. We create opportunities for young people to learn, participate, take responsibility and build the confidence, skills and purpose needed to shape their futures.

FROM OUR FOUNDER

A Journey Built Around People, Purpose & Hope

Driven by a deep passion for mental health and human potential, Ken has dedicated his work to creating spaces where people can learn, grow, connect and thrive. Through Angaza, he has championed mental health awareness, community empowerment and inclusive wellness initiatives, reaching individuals and communities across Kenya.

Beyond his work with Angaza, Ken is an author, public speaker, corporate trainer, mentor and leadership advocate. Through his books, talks, trainings and community engagements, he explores themes of mental wellness, leadership, personal development, resilience and purposeful living.

Over the years, Ken has emerged as a thoughtful voice in conversations around mental health, leadership and personal transformation in Kenya, consistently encouraging individuals and organizations to take ownership of their growth and contribute meaningfully to the communities around them.

Ken Kayange Founder, Angaza Wellness Foundation
