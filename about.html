DOING LIFE TOGETHER

About Us

Angaza Wellness Foundation is a community-based nonprofit organization committed to improving mental health and overall well-being through education, awareness, prevention, counselling, psychosocial support, mentorship, advocacy, and community empowerment.

Our story

Founded by counsellors, educators, and community advocates, Angaza Wellness Foundation began as a movement to bring mental health conversations out of the shadows in Kenya. The word “Angaza” means “to shine” or “to illuminate” — and that is exactly what we strive to do for minds, hearts, and futures.

We believe that everyone deserves access to compassionate mental health support regardless of their background. To date, Angaza has directly impacted over 100,000 people through counselling, safe spaces, our flagship EWIP program, advocacy campaigns, and structured mentorship for young people.

Every session, circle, and mentorship conversation is another step in Doing Life Together — walking with our people toward healing, growth, and hope.

Our values

These principles guide every decision we make and every relationship we build.

Compassion

We meet every person with empathy, respect, and a genuine commitment to their wellbeing.

Community

We believe lasting change happens when communities lead the way and own the solutions.

Integrity

Hope

We work to illuminate possibility — even in the hardest places — so wellness can flourish.
