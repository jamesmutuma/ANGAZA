OUR APPROACH

Three Pillars That Guide Our Work

Our work is built around three interconnected pillars that help individuals and communities move towards sustainable wellbeing.

01

Education

Equipping individuals and communities with knowledge and practical information that promotes mental wellness and healthy living.

02

03

FROM OUR FOUNDER

A Journey Built Around People, Purpose & Hope

Ken Kayange is the Founder of Angaza Wellness Foundation and a passionate advocate for mental wellness and community development.

With years of experience in leadership, community engagement, and organizational development, Ken has been committed to creating spaces where people can feel heard, supported, and empowered to take positive steps in their lives.

Through Angaza, his vision is to bring people together, strengthen communities, and make conversations around mental wellness more accessible and meaningful.
