Our Programs

From mental health support to youth mentorship, our programs are built around three pillars — Education, Advocacy, and Mentorship — to nurture whole-person wellness.

Mental Health &
Psychosocial Support

We offer individual counselling, group therapy, corporate therapy, pre-marital counselling, school wellness programs, and team building — meeting people wherever they are on their wellness journey.

  • Individual counselling
  • Group & corporate therapy
  • Pre-marital counselling
  • School wellness programs
  • Team building

Empowerment & Wellness Integration Program (EWIP)

Our flagship program empowers generations by equipping them with practical skills that open real pathways to careers, purpose, and self-sufficiency.

  • Skills development
  • Career pathways
  • Wellness integration

Safe Spaces

Angaza creates safe spaces for people to connect, vent, and talk about how they feel — offering compassionate emotional support and community belonging.

  • Peer connection circles
  • Confidential conversations
  • Emotional support

Advocacy

Mental health challenges are alarmingly prevalent in Kenya. We create awareness, break stigma, and advocate for accessible, dignified mental health care for all.

  • Awareness campaigns
  • Stigma reduction
  • Community outreach

Youth Mentorship Programs

Angaza invests time and knowledge in young people — walking with them as they grow, discover their potential, and build the confidence to shape their futures.

  • One-on-one mentorship
  • Group mentorship circles
  • Life-skills coaching

How we deliver our programs

Every program begins with listening. We work with schools, workplaces, community leaders, and young people themselves to understand what mental wellness and empowerment mean in their context.

From there, we co-design safe, culturally grounded interventions — combining professional therapy, peer support, skills training, and mentorship — so healing and growth continue long after our direct involvement.
