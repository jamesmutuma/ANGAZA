The Angaza Gala Dinner 2026 Is Finally Happening

An evening of purpose, connection, generosity and Doing Life Together. The wait is finally over. The Angaza Gala Dinner 2026 is happening!

We are incredibly excited about this moment. Our hearts are filled with joy as we look forward to an evening that is not only about celebration, but also about purpose, connection and making a difference together. For Angaza, this gala represents much more than a dinner. It is an opportunity to bring people together around a cause that continues to grow in urgency: the mental wellness and empowerment of our communities.

The Need Is Greater Than Ever

Every day, the world continues to call for help. At Angaza, we have always been willing to step in and respond to these needs. However, the reality of limited resources often means that our hands are tied, even when we know there is more we could do.

Prisons are calling for mental health support. Young people are calling for mentorship, guidance and opportunities to discover their potential. Communities on the other hand are looking for opportunities to be empowered, equipped and supported to build better lives.

There is so much work to be done. And we believe that we do not have to do it alone.

Why the Angaza Gala Dinner Matters

The Angaza Gala Dinner 2026 provides an opportunity for people who believe in what we do to stand with us and support the work directly.

The evening will help create greater awareness around the work Angaza is doing while mobilising resources to strengthen and expand our community-based mental health and empowerment initiatives.

Our work is built around a simple belief: local challenges require local solutions.

Through our programs, Angaza continues to create spaces where people can learn, connect, receive support, discover opportunities and grow. The gala gives us an opportunity to bring more people into this journey.

What to Expect

The Angaza Gala is not simply a moment for people to give. It is also a moment to connect.

We are bringing together thought leaders, executives, professionals, entrepreneurs, community leaders, mental health advocates, partners, friends and people from different fields who share an interest in creating healthier, stronger and more empowered communities.

We believe that some of the most meaningful solutions begin with conversations. A conversation can become a partnership. A partnership can become a program. A program can change a life. We also believe that a group of people committed to a common purpose can transform a community. This is the spirit we want to create at the Angaza Gala Dinner.

What Your Support Will Help Us Do

The resources mobilised through the gala will contribute to strengthening Angaza's ability to respond to the growing needs within communities.

Among the areas we are seeking to strengthen are:

  1. Community mental health awareness and education
  2. Individual mental health support and safe spaces
  3. Youth mentorship and empowerment
  4. Community outreach initiatives
  5. Coverage of mental health support within institutions and vulnerable communities
  6. Skills development and empowerment opportunities
  7. Expansion of Angaza Wellness Centers and community-based initiatives
  8. Development of sustainable programs that can reach more people

Our ambition is not simply to reach more people, but to build systems and programs that can continue creating impact long after a single event is over.

Scaling Up? Yes, That’s the Answer

Angaza Wellness Foundation is entering a new chapter and the signs of growth are becoming increasingly difficult to ignore.

Whether Angaza is scaling up or not is supposed to be the question, but apparently it is the answer.

Speaking to stakeholders and members of the Angaza community, the Founder and Executive Director of Angaza Wellness Foundation, Mr. Ken Kayange, confirmed that Angaza is indeed scaling up—perhaps to levels that had not initially been foreseen.

According to Ken, the expansion of Angaza’s volunteer base, the onboarding of a new seven-member Board of Directors, and the significant resource mobilisation currently underway are some of the clearest signs that the organization is entering a new phase.

“Angaza is no longer for play,” Ken said, reflecting on the growing structures, commitments and expectations around the organization.

He noted that all seven Board members, together with the management team and the wider Angaza fraternity, are working towards the realization of what has come to be known as the Angaza Dream.

"Just fasten your belts and see Angaza unfold."
Ken Kayange, Founder & Executive Director

Five Years Of Doing Life Together

Angaza has now been working within Kenyan communities for approximately five years.

What began as an idea has grown into a community-driven organization with an expanding network of volunteers, programs, partners and supporters

Ken believes the organization has reached a point of maturity that calls for a bigger vision and a greater level of investment in its work.

He described the journey so far as a test of time—one that Angaza has managed to navigate despite operating with limited resources.

For an organization built largely around volunteerism and community participation, reaching more than 100,000 people directly is a milestone that the Angaza team considers significant.

“We have done a commendable job as an organization. Reaching a record of over 100,000 people directly impacted with the limited resources we have had is something to be proud of. We are only looking forward to a brighter future,” Ken said.

A New Chapter Is Already Taking Shape

The growth is not only visible in numbers.

Over the past year, Angaza has been putting greater emphasis on strengthening its internal structures, leadership, programs and resource mobilisation efforts.

The onboarding of seven Board members has brought additional expertise and leadership into the organization, while the expanding volunteer community continues to provide the energy and human capital behind much of Angaza’s work.

At the same time, the organization is exploring and developing initiatives designed to create more sustainable opportunities for communities. The goal is clear: to build an Angaza that is stronger, more structured and capable of reaching many more people.

The Founder Has Been a Busy Man

For Ken Kayange, the year has been one of building, fixing and preparing. Much of his focus has been on strengthening the organization from within and ensuring that Angaza is positioned for its next stage of growth.

Some of the fruits of this work are already beginning to show. One of the most significant milestones has been the acquisition of Angaza’s first-ever physical office—a development that marks an important transition for an organization that has spent much of its journey operating through community spaces, volunteer networks and shared platforms.

The office represents more than a physical space. It is a sign of a growing institution. It provides a place from which the team can coordinate its work, engage stakeholders and build systems that can support Angaza's expanding ambitions.

So, Is Angaza Scaling Up?

The answer appears to be yes.

The question now is not whether Angaza is growing, but how far this growth can go—and what can be built from the foundation laid over the past five years.

With a growing volunteer base, a new Board, increased resource mobilisation, stronger internal structures and a physical home for the organization, Angaza is preparing for what could be its most significant chapter yet.

For the Angaza community, there is a sense that something bigger is taking shape.

And as Ken puts it:

“Just fasten your belts and see Angaza unfold.”

The journey continues.
